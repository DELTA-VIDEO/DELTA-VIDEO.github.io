DELTA VIDEOLearning Residual Updates
for Long-Term Video Memory

Anonymous authors · Under review at ICLR 2027

Delta Video is a learnable online memory mechanism for causal streaming video generation. It maintains a fixed-size recurrent state, writes frame-wise prediction residuals, and retrieves historical context through query- and output-side readout heads. Redundancy-aware supervision stabilizes memory states and readouts under redundant histories.
